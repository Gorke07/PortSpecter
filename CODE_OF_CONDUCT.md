# Community conduct

PortSpecter welcomes constructive contributions in English and Turkish. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed discussions.

- Treat participants respectfully, regardless of background or experience.
- Discuss the work and evidence; avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats and discriminatory language.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information, credentials or network data.
- Keep reports and reviews relevant and actionable. Accept reasonable moderation requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation to protect the project community. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For a conduct concern, use GitHub's reporting controls where available. If private maintainer contact is needed, request a private channel without posting personal details publicly. No dedicated conduct-reporting email or response SLA is currently published.
